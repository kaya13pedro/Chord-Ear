Put the phone or iPad near the speaker. Chord Ear finds the beat from the drums and strums, then reads the harmony of the whole band (guitars, keys, bass) and only changes chord on a beat, once the new chord has lasted at least the length you set. It shows the harmony, so a guitar playing a different voicing still lines up with the chord names. Bars count from the first chord change and assume four beats per bar.
