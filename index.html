Shapes

Tap a shape to hear it. Orange dots are the root.

After a few strums Chord Ear finds the beat and only changes chord on a beat, once the new chord has lasted at least the length you set under Rhythm. Bar lines start from the first chord change. Without a steady beat it waits for about a second of the new chord.

Chords that share the same notes, like Am7 and C6 or Asus4 and Dsus2, are told apart by the lowest note; the other reading shows under “Could also be”.
