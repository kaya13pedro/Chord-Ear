Put the phone near the speaker and tap Listen. The chord name comes from what the wheel shows. The triad is the chord’s three main notes on the D, G and B strings, placed as close as possible to the last one so your hand barely moves. Dashed rings are the chord’s extra colour notes, like the ♭7 in a m7 chord. Next? is a guess from what followed this chord earlier in the song. Tap the whole neck to move to another spot; tap the wheel to hold a chord. If the chord flickers between two names, set Chord changes to Steady.
